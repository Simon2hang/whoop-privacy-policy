# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Personal WHOOP Health Assistant  
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

## 1. Overview

Personal WHOOP Health Assistant is a personal-use application designed to retrieve and analyze health and fitness data from WHOOP with the account owner's authorization.

## 2. Information Collected

With authorization, the application may access:

- Recovery scores, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate
- Sleep duration, sleep stages, and sleep performance
- Daily strain and physiological activity metrics
- Workout and exercise information

## 3. How Information Is Used

The information is used solely to generate personal health, recovery, sleep, and fitness summaries and recommendations.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Access credentials and health information will be stored using access-controlled systems. Sensitive credentials will not be intentionally published in public repositories. Access is limited to the application owner and services explicitly authorized to process the data.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The application may use WHOOP's API, GitHub Actions, and other explicitly configured services for authorized data processing and report delivery. Data will only be shared with services necessary to provide the requested functionality.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data will be retained only as needed for the application's functionality. The account owner may discontinue authorization and request deletion of application-controlled stored data.

## 7. User Consent

The application accesses WHOOP information only after the account owner grants authorization through WHOOP's OAuth process.

## 8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the application owner using the contact email provided in the WHOOP Developer Dashboard.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality, data handling, or service providers.
